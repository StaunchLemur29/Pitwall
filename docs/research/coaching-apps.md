# Sim-racing coaching and telemetry products: survey for Pitwall

Researched 2026-11-04. **About the sources:** page fetching was blocked in this session (the vendor domains don't resolve here), so every fact below comes from search-result extracts of the cited pages. Prices and sim lists change often and several sources disagree; where they do, I say so. Vendor claims are marked as vendor claims, and user opinions are marked **(user opinion)**.

## The products

### Trophi.ai (Driver61 / Scott Mansell)
- **What it does:** an AI coach with a live voice coach called "Mansell AI", which gives live technique analysis, skill grading and brake-point help through your headset in 59+ languages. It also has on-screen telemetry overlays and AI reports after each session. [pricing](https://www.trophi.ai/pricing), [live overlays](https://www.trophi.ai/sim-racing-features/live-telemetry-overlays)
- **How it coaches:** in real time (voice and overlay) and after the session (reports). The Professional tier adds a monthly 1-to-1 session with a human coach. [iRacing page](https://www.trophi.ai/sim-racing-coaching/iracing)
- **Sims:** iRacing, ACC, F1 23/24/25 and Le Mans Ultimate. **Neither the original Assetto Corsa nor AC EVO appears on its pages.** [get.trophi.ai](https://get.trophi.ai)
- **Price:** Premium $12.99/mo, Premium + Setups $19.99/mo, Professional $69.99/mo. 7-day trial and a 30-day refund guarantee. Third-party sites quote other figures. [pricing](https://www.trophi.ai/pricing)
- **Opinions:** Trustpilot scores it about 2.8–3/5 (user opinion; the extracts didn't show the review text). [Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/trophi.ai) Top Gear's opinion piece makes a general point about AI coaches: they work from telemetry alone and "can't see what you can see", so they can't give you visual reference points. [Top Gear](https://www.topgear.com/car-news/gaming/opinion-can-ai-make-you-a-better-driver)

### Track Titan
- **What it does:** automatic analysis of your laps against reference laps, "Coaching Flows" that take you through a lap's braking, throttle and line errors one at a time, a 3D replay, leaderboards and setups that install automatically. [Nov 2025 update](https://www.tracktitan.io/post/november-2025-update-coaching-flows), [3D replay](https://www.tracktitan.io/post/new-track-titan-feature-launch-introducing-3d-replay)
- **Real time:** a live overlay shows your throttle and brake beside a reference lap, and the reference updates every time you go faster. It also gives short tips such as "You are braking too late". [RTO](https://www.tracktitan.io/rto), [Oct 2025 update](https://www.tracktitan.io/post/october-2025-track-titan-update-new-live-overlays-for-all-sims)
- **Sims:** the help centre lists Assetto Corsa among the supported games. Coaching Flows launched for iRacing, ACC and LMU only, with "more to come". [help](https://intercom.help/track-titan/en/articles/6227990-what-games-are-supported-by-track-titan)
- **Price:** a free Community tier. Paid tiers start around $7.99/mo for AI tips and pro reference laps (third-party figure). [topai.tools](https://topai.tools/t/track-titan)
- **Opinions:** users praise the telemetry comparison against others and the leaderboards (user opinion). One user asks what it's useful for if you aren't very good yet, and another says it "takes time and application" (user opinion). [Trustpilot](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/www.tracktitan.io), [RaceDepartment](https://www.racedepartment.com/threads/track-titan-free-lap-data-analysis-for-the-simracing-community.212446/) The company claims a 0.5 s average gain after one analysis (vendor claim). The company raised $5M in Dec 2025 and partners with Moza. [tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2025/12/04/track-titan-raises-5m-for-ai-powered-strava-for-motorsport/)

### Coach Dave Academy: Delta
- **What it does:** setups that install themselves, lap tracking, telemetry, session recordings, live telemetry (since v3.0) and "Auto Insights" AI coaching. [Delta](https://coachdaveacademy.com/delta/), [Traxion](https://traxion.gg/native-telemetry-tracking-and-analysis-added-to-coach-daves-delta-service/)
- **How Auto Insights coaches:** it splits **each corner into four phases (braking, entry, apex, exit)**, judges each phase against a reference lap and colours it from green to red. Its own advice is to **"pick the one or two corners with the most red"** for your next run, and to use a reference lap **0.5–1.5 s faster** than you ("too far and everything shows as a loss"). It starts after your out-lap, needs 2–3 clean laps and works in live and past sessions. [how-to](https://coachdaveacademy.com/documentation/how-to-use-auto-insights-ai-coaching-in-delta/), [tutorial](https://coachdaveacademy.com/tutorials/ai-sim-racing-coaching-unlock-faster-lap-times-with-delta-ai-auto-insights/)
- **Sims:** iRacing, ACC, LMU, GT7, AMS2, **AC EVO, and AC (added in 2.2)**. The original AC has a slightly reduced feature set and no full setup support. [Delta 2.2](https://boxthislap.org/delta-2-2-is-now-compatible-with-le-mans-ultimate-and-assetto-corsa/), [AC EVO doc](https://coachdaveacademy.com/documentation/delta-support-for-ac-evo-daily-racing/)
- **Price:** £11.99/mo or £109/yr (shown as $/€ in some regions). [boxthislap](https://boxthislap.org/new-subscription-for-coach-dave-academys-delta/)
- **Opinions:** no independent reviews found. Almost all material is Coach Dave's own, including its comparison pages against its competitors.

### VRS (Virtual Racing School)
- **What it does:** a web-based telemetry viewer, a TelemetryLogger, "datapacks" (a pro's setup, telemetry and replay for each car and track), side-by-side video comparison and human 1-to-1 coaching in three tiers. [FAQ](https://virtualracingschool.com/faq/), [pricing](https://virtualracingschool.com/pricing/)
- **How it coaches:** after the session, by comparing yourself with a pro's reference. An older review described a "virtual coach" algorithm that compares your laps with your best lap.
- **Sims:** traditionally iRacing-centric. Sources conflict on the rest: one Coach Dave page says iRacing only, another lists AC, AC EVO, GT7 and AMS2. **Unconfirmed for AC.** [comparison](https://coachdaveacademy.com/tutorials/coach-dave-delta-vs-vrs-which-one-is-best-for-you/)
- **Price:** a free "Casual" tier with time-limited datapack access and no advanced telemetry. The paid price wasn't visible in the extracts. [pricing](https://virtualracingschool.com/pricing/)

### Garage 61
- **What it does:** records every lap and lets you compare it with other drivers' laps in the same car. The community shares ghost laps, telemetry and setups. **It gives no coaching**: "you need to read the telemetry yourself". [Coach Dave comparison](https://coachdaveacademy.com/tutorials/coach-dave-delta-vs-garage-61-which-is-best-for-you/)
- **Sims:** iRacing (per that competitor's comparison, which may be dated).
- **Price:** free. Pro costs about $6.50–7/mo through Patreon and adds filters, telemetry on other drivers' laps and longer storage.
- **Opinions:** a "Garage 61 is awesome" thread (user opinion). The developer declined to build a reference made from the best sectors of several drivers, saying it's "not really something I'd recommend". [r/iRacing](https://reddit.birdcat.cafe/r/iRacing/comments/1gu3dyf/garage_61_is_awesome) Third-party tools add coaching on top of its data, which suggests users want it interpreted. [Firefox add-on](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/rb-telemetry-coach/)

### MoTeC i2 and AC-native exporters
- **MoTeC i2 Pro:** free for non-professional use. It is a pro-grade data analysis tool (channels, maths, overlays) with **no coaching**, and it's hard to learn. [overtake.gg](https://overtake.gg/threads/acc-blog-motec-telemetry-and-dedicated-acc-workspace.165714)
- **For AC:** ACTI writes AC data as i2 logs, but users call it "quite abandoned" (user opinion). [RaceDepartment](https://www.racedepartment.com/downloads/acti-assetto-corsa-telemetry-interface.3948) **Telemetrick** is a CSP Lua app (CSP ≥0.2.4) that exports CSV or MoTeC files and ships with an i2 workspace. [overtake.gg](https://overtake.gg/downloads/telemetrick.73265)

### SimHub
- **What it does:** a dashboard and overlay builder (Dash Studio), bass shakers, LEDs and motion. You choose what to pay for a perpetual licence (from about €5), which unlocks 60 fps dashboards. Motion needs a separate licence. **It doesn't coach.** [licence](https://www.simhubdash.com/get-a-license/)
- **Sims:** 80+, **including AC, ACC and AC EVO (PC)**. [supported games](https://www.simhubdash.com/supported-games/)

### RaceLab
- **What it does:** racing overlays (relative, standings, fuel, inputs, VR overlays) and a Stream Deck plugin. It is free with a Pro upgrade; prices weren't visible. AC is listed among its sims. **It doesn't coach.** [racelab.app](https://racelab.app/), [iRacing page](https://racelab.app/iracing)

### Others worth knowing
- **Moza "Racing Lab":** AI racing-line and reference-driving tools for AC and ACC on a limited set of cars and tracks. Replacing the AI line works in AC only. [Moza support](https://support.mozaracing.com/en/support/solutions/articles/70000683725-what-is-racing-lab-and-how-do-i-use-it-)
- **AC voice engineers:** PitWise ($3.99) lets you press a wheel button, ask a question and hear an answer from live telemetry. It covers AC, includes braking and tyre coaching and works offline. **Nuzzi Race Engineer** is a CSP Lua app that uses Windows speech recognition. **AC Pro Coach** (itch.io) works from AC's shared memory. [itch.io](https://b4ben.itch.io/ac-pro-coach), [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/posts/135859711)
- **ACTracker** (in development) runs in the background and has an AI coach for after the session. **RaceIQ** is open source and pitches itself as a free alternative to Track Titan and Delta. [ACTracker](https://ac-tracker-explanation.pages.dev/), [RaceIQ](https://git.huazai-net.workers.dev/SpeedHQ/RaceIQ)
- **Sim Racing Telemetry (iOS):** records laps from AC and other games for review on the phone. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sim-racing-telemetry/id1288695792)

## Gaps Pitwall can fill
1. **Proper AC coaching is thin.** Trophi doesn't support AC, and Track Titan's Coaching Flows don't cover it. Delta supports AC with a reduced feature set. None of them handles **modded cars and tracks** (RSS, Nordschleife variants) well, because each one is built around reference laps from pros or the community, and those don't exist for a heavily modded install.
2. **Everyone depends on references from other drivers.** Delta's own advice (a reference 0.5–1.5 s faster, otherwise "everything shows as a loss") shows the weak spot. Pitwall's simulated optimal run at the car's learnt grip gives a reference that is *reachable* for any mod combination and sets its own distance to the driver.
3. **Drills are missing everywhere.** No product found does "retry this section" with a teleport. They all analyse whole laps.
4. **Track Titan users ask what to do if they aren't good yet** (user opinion). Raw telemetry such as Garage 61 or MoTeC needs interpreting. A single next action fixes that, and suits ADHD.
5. **Nordschleife-length laps** turn whole-lap review into noise. Splitting the lap into sections and drills fits better than any lap-centric tool.
6. **AI coaches can't see visual references** (Top Gear). Pitwall knows the track geometry and the driver's own braking points, so it can name a *place* ("brake at the 150 board", or the distance) rather than only a delta.

## What this means for Pitwall
Ranked by usefulness. The status in brackets says whether Pitwall has it.

1. **One focus per session, chosen for him** *(partly: "Good and bad" review and time to beat).* Borrow Delta's rule of "fix one or two reddest corners" and Coaching Flows' step-by-step walk through a lap, but narrow it to **one corner and one phase**. The debrief ends with "next session: T7 braking", and the live card emphasises only that corner.
2. **Score each corner by phase: braking, entry, apex, exit** *(not yet as a fixed split; Pitwall has per-corner analysis).* Delta's four phases are a simple, readable model. Show only the worst phase per corner, so it never becomes a grid of numbers.
3. **Keep the reference reachable** *(has it: the optimal run at learnt grip).* Make it explicit as a target a set distance ahead of his current best, so it stays "a bit faster than me" and not a pro lap. Self-adjusting like this is something no competitor can do for mods.
4. **Turn findings straight into drills** *(drills exist).* No competitor has drills. Link them up so the debrief's one focus offers "drill this section ×5" with the teleport ready. This is Pitwall's clearest edge.
5. **Short spoken cues, opt-in** *(not yet; visual cues exist).* Trophi and the AC voice engineers show people want audio while driving. Make it **one short phrase per corner, only for the focus corner**, never a running commentary. The brake countdown could become a tone. It works without looking at the side screen, which matters in a dense Nordschleife section.
6. **Reference updates itself as he improves** *(likely partly).* Track Titan's live overlay refreshes the reference each time you go faster. Pitwall's time to beat should update quietly and say so once ("new target T7: −0.12").
7. **Concrete places, not just deltas** *(partly: brake cues).* Phrase advice by landmark or distance-to-corner, which answers the "AI can't see" criticism.
8. **Name a real result** *(the hub has progress).* Track Titan sells "0.5 s after one analysis". Show the driver what a drill earned him ("T7 braking: +0.18 s since Tuesday"), measured against himself, which fits "get better" without social comparison.
9. **MoTeC/CSV export as an escape hatch** *(unknown).* This is cheap if lap data already exists (Telemetrick shows the format). It's low priority and only for deep dives.
10. **Leave out:** leaderboards and community ghosts (comparing with others isn't the goal), setup marketplaces, overlays full of widgets like RaceLab or SimHub, and copying any product's HUD styling (the owner turned that down). Leave out human-coach tiers too.
